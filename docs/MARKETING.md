# Marketing Copy — Fundraising Intelligence Platform

## One-Line Value Proposition

**Find the right funders. Make the case. Get funded.**

---

## Elevator Pitch

Your organization does important work — but a generic grant application doesn't show that. This platform turns your real data (financials, impact metrics, team capacity) into a transparent, verifiable presentation that foundations can actually evaluate. It's built for purpose-driven organizations that are serious about fundraising but tired of spending weeks on applications that don't reflect how good their work actually is. Foundations receive hundreds of identical submissions — when yours shows genuine mission alignment, cites every number, and adapts its story to each funder's priorities, it doesn't just stand out. It wins.

---

## Feature Benefits

- **You can find which foundations are actually worth your effort.** Browse a scored, prioritized database of researched foundations — each rated for mission fit, application readiness, and strategic priority — so you stop chasing long shots and focus where funding is most likely.

- **You can generate a tailored, professionally designed grant application for any foundation in minutes.** Not a template with blanks to fill — a real document built from your impact data, adapted to the foundation's themes, with a matching budget and narrative that speaks their language.

- **You can share a live, click-through presentation of your organization with any program officer, where every number links to its source.** No "trust us" claims. Foundations evaluate you with the same confidence they'd give an audited report.

---

## Call to Action

**Bring your annual report and your mission. We'll show you who should fund it.**

---

## Objections and Answers

**"We already have a process — spreadsheets, email, it works."**
It works until a key person leaves, a deadline slips, or you apply to the same foundation twice with different numbers. A shared pipeline means nothing falls through the cracks. Your institutional knowledge stays in the system, not in someone's head.

**"Our funders use their own forms anyway."**
Most do — and this doesn't replace their form. It replaces the weeks of research, story drafting, and number-checking that happen before you even open their form. You arrive prepared, with a clear narrative and the right data already organized.

**"We're too small / don't have the bandwidth to set this up."**
Setup is a document drop, not a project. Hand over your statutes, annual report, and strategy. The platform reads them and configures itself for your organization. There's no drawn-out IT implementation — you're running your first foundation research the same day.
